Make the buffer visible

Frequently asked questions

Backward planning questions

How is backward planning different from a countdown?

A countdown shows time remaining. Backward planning subtracts several required durations to find when you should start.

Where should I add buffer time?

Add buffer as its own step so you can see how much safety margin the plan includes.

Can the result fall on the previous day?

Yes. If the target is early and the plan is long, the result clearly shows yesterday or the earlier date.
