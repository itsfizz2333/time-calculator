FAQ

Time-from-now questions

How do I find what time it will be in 90 minutes?

Tap +90 min, or enter 90 in the Minutes field. The calculator adds 1 hour and 30 minutes to your current local time.

What happens if the future time crosses midnight?

The result automatically moves to the correct next calendar date.

Can I enter more than 59 minutes?

Yes. Values such as 90 or 150 minutes are accepted and normalized automatically.

Which time zone does the calculator use?

It uses the local date and time reported by your device. It does not convert between time zones.

Does the starting time update automatically?

Yes. The current time refreshes each minute, and you can also use Refresh now.
