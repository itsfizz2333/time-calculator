The formula

End time − start time − break

Convert each clock time to minutes after midnight. Add 1,440 minutes when the range crosses midnight, subtract the break, then divide the result by 60.

FAQ

Time duration questions

How do I calculate the hours between two times?

Convert both times to minutes after midnight and subtract the start from the end. This calculator performs the conversion automatically.

Can this handle a shift that crosses midnight?

Yes. When the end time is earlier than the start time, the calculator treats the end as occurring on the next day.

Can I subtract a lunch break?

Yes. Enter the break in minutes and it will be subtracted from the elapsed time.

What are decimal hours?

Decimal hours express minutes as a fraction of an hour. For example, 7 hours and 15 minutes equals 7.25 hours.

What happens if the break is longer than the time range?

The result is limited to zero and the calculator displays a warning so the inputs can be corrected.
