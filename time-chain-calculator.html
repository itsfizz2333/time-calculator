Frequently asked questions

Multi-step time questions

How are multiple durations added?

Each step starts when the previous one ends, so the entire timeline and final finish time update automatically.

What happens if I change a step in the middle?

Every later start and finish time is recalculated immediately.

Can I reorder activities?

Yes. Drag on a computer or use the move buttons on a phone or keyboard.
